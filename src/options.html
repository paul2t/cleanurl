CleanURL

Trackers are stripped from the request before the page loads, and from links as you copy them.

Cleaning

Turns every rule below on or off.

Tidies what is left after the page loads, including trackers a site adds to the URL itself.

Covers share buttons, right-click copy, Ctrl+C over selected text, and drag and drop.

Turns google.com/url?q=… and l.instagram.com/?u=… into the destination when copied.

Off by default: these pay the person who shared the link, so removing them is a choice rather than a fix.

Exceptions

One domain per line. Subdomains are included.
